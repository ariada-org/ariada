# S1: Finish the Shopify app to listing-grade

The proposed Shopify deliverable would be an app and Shopify App Store listing that merchants can discover, authorize for a development store, and evaluate against their Online Store content before adopting it.

## Boundary

No installable or listed Shopify app is claimed; a future app would report on storefront pages made available to it, not change themes, publish products, approve checkout behavior, or guarantee that Shopify will accept the listing.

## Roles

- Shopify Partner submission lead
- Merchant storefront accessibility owner

## Use cases

- A Shopify Partner would prepare the listing copy, demo store, privacy disclosures, and requested scopes for Shopify's app review.
- A merchant would trial the app on an unpublished theme and inspect findings for product, collection, cart, and theme-template previews before pushing the theme live.

## Installation

Not installable: Finish the Shopify app to listing-grade has no qualifying public implementation or package in the canonical Ariada repository.
