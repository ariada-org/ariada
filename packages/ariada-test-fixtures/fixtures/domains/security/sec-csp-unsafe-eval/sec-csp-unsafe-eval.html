CSP: default-src 'self' 'unsafe-eval' — 'unsafe-eval' in the fallback directive propagates to script-src.

CSP: script-src 'self' — no 'unsafe-eval' token present.

CSP: script-src 'strict-dynamic' 'nonce-abc123' — no 'unsafe-eval' token.
