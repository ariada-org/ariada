Site without llms.txt

This site does not publish an llms.txt file at the root of its domain. LLM agents that follow the llms.txt convention (introduced by Answer.AI in September 2024) will have no machine-readable guide to which content on this site they are permitted to read and cite.

Without llms.txt, AI assistants must guess from robots.txt or their own policies whether to use this site's content in generated responses.
