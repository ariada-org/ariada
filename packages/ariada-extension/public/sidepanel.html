What to check

Accessibility and privacy findings cite the law that requires them. The rest are good practice: AI readiness, structured data and sustainability cite recommendations, not requirements, and stay out of a conformance claim.

Additional sites

This panel scans the page you opened it from. A page from another site cannot be fetched from the browser, so use the command-line tool to scan more addresses.

Why ariada

  • 100 % local — no data leaves your browser
  • Scans localhost, staging, and authenticated pages
  • Five checks in one pass over the page — accessibility and privacy against the law, plus AI readiness, structured data and sustainability as good practice. Transport security needs response headers, which a page cannot read, so the command-line tool does it.
  • Detects cross-domain conflicts (e.g. privacy fix hiding a labelled control)
  • Free and open source (EUPL-1.2)
